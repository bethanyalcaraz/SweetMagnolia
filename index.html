What's inside

Real ingredients you can pronounce

We bake the way grandma did, with butter and oats, and we whip our marshmallow cream on the stovetop every baking day.

The cookies

  • Old-fashioned rolled oats
  • Real butter
  • Brown sugar & a touch of molasses
  • Cinnamon & vanilla
  • Flour, eggs, sea salt

The marshmallow cream

  • Fresh egg whites
  • Cane sugar syrup, cooked to soft-ball
  • Pure vanilla
  • Whipped from scratch, never from a jar

What we leave out

  • Canola & vegetable oil
  • Soybean & cottonseed oil
  • Shortening & margarine
  • Jarred marshmallow fluff
  • Preservatives

Allergens: contains wheat, dairy, and eggs. Baked in a home kitchen that also handles nuts.

Our story

A family stand under the palm trees

Sweet Magnolia started in our kitchen with one craving: the oatmeal cream pies we grew up on, without the ingredient list we couldn't read. After a lot of test batches (and a lot of happy taste testers), we landed on a cookie that's soft in the middle, crisp at the edge, and big enough to share.

Now you'll find us most weekends under the white tent with the pink tablecloth. The kids hand out samples, we pack your box, and every pie goes home made with love.

Questions

Good to know

What does "seed-oil free" mean?

We never use canola, vegetable, soybean, sunflower, safflower, cottonseed, or corn oil, and no shortening or margarine. Our cookies get their richness from real butter.

How long do the cream pies stay fresh?

They're best within 3 days at room temperature in their wrapper, or up to a week in the fridge. Let them sit out 15 minutes before eating for the softest bite. They also freeze well for a month.

Can I order for a party or event?

Yes. The party tray has 24 mini cream pies, and we can do custom ribbon colors and tags for birthdays, showers, and teacher gifts. Add details in the notes at checkout, or order at least one week ahead for 4+ dozen.

Do you ship?

Not yet. Marshmallow cream is happiest close to home, so we offer pickup at the tent and local delivery only.
